How it works

Light starts a color cycle. Press to freeze, then step through eight colors before turning off.

Sleep uses low mist and switches the light off. Press again to restore the previous mist setting and light. Changing Light during Sleep keeps your new light setting.

Mist toggles regular mist and exits Sleep. It can return low mist to high or off, depending on the remembered regular setting.

Timer turns both light and mist off after one minute. Press again to cancel.

Water lasts 3 minutes on high or 6 on low. Hold refill for 5 seconds to fill an empty tank. Empty water stops mist; light stays as it is.
